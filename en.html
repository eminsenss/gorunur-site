Google My Business, Business Profile… are they the same thing?

Yes. They are older and newer names for the same thing: the card that shows your business on Google Maps and Search. We manage it with your permission, and it always stays yours.

My business is not on Google Maps. Can you add it?

Yes, on Google, Apple and Yandex. The maps charge nothing for a listing; we charge a one-off setup fee. Verification is done by the map provider and can take a few days. Details (Turkish) →

I am not good with computers. Can I still use it?

Yes. You only tap the button in the message on your phone. We do the setup together in a few minutes.

Do you write fake reviews or get bad reviews removed?

No. We draft a calm, polite reply to a review you find unfair, and nothing is published until you approve it.

Are you connected to Google?

No. We are an independent service and only use the manager access you give us.
